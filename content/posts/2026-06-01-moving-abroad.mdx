It's been eight months since I first lived somewhere outside home. I moved from Spain all the way to
Romania for a whole academic year. There is absolutely no argument that would deny me the fact that it
has been the best eight months of my life. 

At first, you are scared of going out there, completely normal. The first thought I got after living abroad
for a month was: "how lucky I am". And there comes the first learning I got, I should be grateful every single
day for what I have been given. Which at some point is obvious, but you don't realize until you see what's out there.
The education, values, goods and so many things we take for granted, which as a whole have built you from scratch.

These things, that we don't often show or say, are the ones that matter. When you are far away, and physical contact
is not possible, you learn to love better, you see everything in another way. Calling your parents and telling them
you love them, is more meaningful than ever, because you have no other way to show it. Same with friends and other
relationships, you become accountable of the things that bring joy to each person around you. I would say this is
one of the big learnings I got, and genuinely believe will influence how my relationships will be in the future.
Don't take nothing for granted, tell people that you love them when you feel it, and appreciate the small
things.

Other skill you develop is to take ownership of your situation, and that gives you freedom. Yes, you have the pressure
to be the one responsible for your own, but that also means you don't depend on others. There are so many learnings 
behind this one, that I can't compact into a sentence. You don't have to get out of your city to experiment this,
but I can tell it is easier. 

For me, socially it was also something insightful. As someone with a very homogeneus social group, meeting all kind
of different people was shocking at first. Multiple nationalities, ideas, situations, you get to see different world
views. You realize the world is actually very diverse, and you become open-minded. No idea is indeed correct and
every opinion can be reformulated into a whole new belief.

I got this feeling of freedom when I took walks. I often go out and wander around the city without a clear destination.
Cable headphones on, 80s music playing, you can even weirdly dance at the street. You have the power of not being
noticed, and you realize no one cares about you, neither home or anywhere. You live in your own movie and you can
do anything you want. I realized I could be fully myself, fearless. You get to spend a lot of time alone, and that
enables you to think thoroughly about yourself, which eventually leads to clarity. 

If only one person reading this, who hasn't ever left home, I encourage you to do so as soon as possible.
It's not a hate declaration to your household, it's the start of your change. I am certain you will become
unrecognisable if you actually embrace the change and become open-minded to see the world differently. You will
discover more things of yourself than ever, only if you are accountable of everything that happens to you.
